# Artist visibility

In Settings → Statistics → Artist visibility, search for an artist in the saved
library and select them to hide their listens from your statistics. Switch an
artist off to show them again; the row stays in the list. Removing a row also
shows the artist again.

Only songs where they are the main artist are excluded. Featured appearances
remain. Preferences belong to each user, including in shared pages and
comparisons. Artist groups count only the members' visible listens.

Direct artist, album and song pages still show their complete saved history.
Artist pages show a notice for hidden artists or hidden members of a group.

This feature is separate from the legacy blacklist. It stores a small preference
list on the user and filters statistics reads before totals and rankings are
calculated. It never changes or deletes listening events, metadata, import
records or blacklist flags. Polling and imports continue to save plays normally.
An artist already on the legacy blacklist remains subject to that separate
setting.
